About FeedForward

FeedForward is a platform that connects generous donors with NGOs and volunteers to reduce waste and extend kindness. Together, we build a community where nothing goes to waste and no one goes hungry.

“
WHAT WE DO

We Facilitate Multiple Forms of Donation

FeedForward supports various types of donations to ensure that help reaches people in the way they need it most.

Food Donation

Share surplus food from events, homes, restaurants and canteens with those who need it.

Clothes Donation

Donate wearable clothes and spread warmth to people in need of basic necessities.

Book Donation

Share your books and learning materials to support education and inspire young minds.

Money Donation

Contribute financially and help NGOs serve more people and create a greater impact.

HOW FEEDFORWARD WORKS

A Simple Process, A Powerful Impact

1

Create Donation

Donors list food, clothes, books or money they wish to donate.

2

Verified by NGO

NGOs review and approve the donation for distribution.

3

Volunteer Assigned

Volunteers are notified and assigned for pickup.

4

Pickup & Delivery

Volunteers pick up the donation and ensure safe delivery.

5

Distributed

Donations are distributed to the right beneficiaries.

6

Impact Created

Lives are improved and kindness continues to grow.
