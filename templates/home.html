HOW FEEDFORWARD WORKS

Innovative Handshake Workflow

Our secure and transparent donation platform makes it easy to donate, verify, collect and deliver help to the people who need it most.

1. Create a Donation

Donors can choose what they want to contribute - food, clothes, books or money - and provide the required donation details.

2. NGO Verification

NGOs review the donation details, verify the contribution and approve suitable donations for people and communities in need.

3. Volunteer Assigned

For physical donations, available volunteers are notified and assigned to collect the donation from the donor.

4. Pickup & Delivery

Volunteers safely collect physical donations and deliver them to the concerned NGO or distribution location.

5. Distribution

NGOs coordinate the distribution of food, clothes and books to the appropriate beneficiaries and communities.

6. Impact Created

Every completed donation contributes to reducing waste, supporting communities and creating a positive social impact.

Disaster Relief & Flood Response

Rapid NGO Activation

NGO partners can quickly claim emergency food requests, assign volunteers, and track delivery status for critical relief missions.
